## Our origin

All three founders were previously CTOs at YC companies. We saw that coding was changing rapidly with agents, so we built the first version of Paradev at a hackathon. Immediately after launching in January, many of our friends switched from their favorite IDEs and used Paradev 8-12 hours a day.

In less than a year, hundreds of thousands of developers in 180 countries and one in eight Fortune 500 companies have used Paradev.

<StoryPhoto />

## Why Paradev

Our old name was shared by many existing products and companies, so we had been looking for a name we can build an enduring company on.

Lawyers have paralegals, doctors have paramedics, and developers have **paradevs**. A parallel dev (or paradev) has hundreds of agents working in parallel, solving issues for her as soon as they come up. Sound familiar?

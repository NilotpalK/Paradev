Engineers used to work with one coding agent at a time. Now one engineer can run dozens at once, each on its own task. Today Superset becomes **Paradev**, short for parallel development, at [paradev.com](https://paradev.com).

We also raised an $11.5M seed round, led by USV with Y Combinator and Paul Graham, to build what comes next.

Nothing changes on your end. Your account, your workspaces and the `superset` command all carry over. The details are at the end of this post.

## Why Paradev

Paradev means parallel development: many agents working at the same time, each in its own workspace. It's what our users do every day, and now our name says it.

We also needed a name that's ours. Many companies and projects are called Superset, including the unrelated Apache Superset. We wanted a name we can build a lasting company on.

## What we've seen

Three of us built the first version at a hackathon last November. When we ran several agents at once, they collided in one checkout, and it was hard to see which one needed us. So we gave every task its own workspace, with its own Git worktree, branch and terminals. It runs on your own machine, with the agents and subscriptions you already have.

In under a year, more than 145,000 developers in 180 countries have created over 1.85 million agent workspaces, and one in eight Fortune 500 companies have used it.

## What's next

Today one engineer runs dozens of agents in parallel. Next, we want every engineering team to have a software factory:

- **Start.** Agents pick up work on a schedule or an event, not only when you type.
- **Run in parallel.** Each one works in its own workspace, with its own Git worktree.
- **Ready to merge.** You come back to reviewed changes, not a queue of half-done branches.

Our most advanced users already work this way. We want every team to. This week we took one step: plugins are now on for every account, so every agent you run in Paradev gets the same skills on every machine.

## What changes

- **The name.** You'll see Paradev in the desktop app, the iPhone app, the docs and our emails.
- **The website.** [paradev.com](https://paradev.com), with docs at [docs.paradev.com](https://docs.paradev.com). Every superset.sh link redirects to the same page on paradev.com.
- **GitHub.** The repo moves to [github.com/paradevai/paradev](https://github.com/paradevai/paradev). Old links and clone URLs redirect.
- **Our accounts.** X [@paradevai](https://x.com/paradevai), LinkedIn [/company/paradevai](https://www.linkedin.com/company/paradevai) and YouTube [@paradevai](https://www.youtube.com/@paradevai). Old links redirect. Same [Discord](https://discord.gg/cZeD9WYcV7) server.
- **Emails.** For a few more weeks, emails still come from superset.sh, with the sender name "Paradev (formerly Superset)". Then they move to paradev.com.
- **Card statements.** Charges show as PARADEV.

## What doesn't change

- The product, its features and the team.
- Your account, workspaces, settings, agents, automations and data. You stay signed in.
- Pricing and billing: same plans, same prices.
- Your integrations, such as GitHub, Slack and Linear. A sign-in or consent screen may show the new name.
- The `superset` command in your terminal and the Homebrew tap. `paradev` now works too, as an alias.

The desktop app updates to the new name on its own. There's nothing to reinstall.
